## A garden instead of slides

This garden collects what we share at the LAÇO meetups: one note per idea, each with live code you can edit and run right on the page.

- **During a meetup**, its page becomes a full-screen present mode with live code. Press **P** there.
- **At home**, browse the notes, change the numbers and see what happens.
- **Over time**, other presenters can add their own notes, so the garden becomes the archive of LAÇO.

## This garden's background

A soft gradient made with Hydra: very slow, very wide stripes, turned on their side and softened. Change a number and press **Run** (or Ctrl/⌘ + Enter):

```hydra
osc(4.5, 0.004, 2.1)
  .rotate(-1.57)
  .saturate(0.6)
  .brightness(0.3)
  .out()
```

If the motion bothers you, use **Pause visuals**. **Hide text** lets you watch the visuals on their own.
